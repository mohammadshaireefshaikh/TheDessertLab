Our story

Born in Liverpool.
Raised on butter.

The Dessert Lab started as a family obsession: could a waffle be crisp at the edge, custard-soft in the middle, and still carry proper single-origin chocolate without collapsing? Forty-three test batches later, Formula №01 was born — and so were we.

Everything here is treated like a recipe worth arguing about. Doughs rest a full 24 hours. Chocolate is couverture-grade and melted to order. Cream comes thick from Cheshire dairies, and nothing leaves the pass until it looks as good as it tastes — because we know the first bite happens with a camera.
